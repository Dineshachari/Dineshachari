# Dinesh Achari

**Systems architect for the neurodivergent mind.** I spent 10 years building systems for financial conglomerates — project plans, scope documents, risk frameworks — while masking ADHD the entire time. Smart enough to manage millions in complexity. Unable to answer a text for three weeks.

```console
$ cat blueprint.status
[survival]  firefighting ....... deprecated
[analysis]  root_cause ......... found
[design]    environment ........ active

$ ./run --from survival --to design
```

The breakthrough wasn't the diagnosis. It was a sentence: **I wasn't running a broken brain — I was running a survival blueprint nobody ever designed.** So I treated my life like a failing project: root-cause analysis, scope minimization, risk mitigation for my own triggers. I took the enterprise frameworks I'd built for banks, stripped the bureaucratic bloat, and rebuilt them for a brain that runs on interest, not obedience.

## What I'm building

**[SuperNudge](https://dineshachari.com/supernudge)** — a native macOS focus companion for ADHD and neurodivergent brains. AI screen-awareness that *nudges* you back to focus with calm ambient cues — never alarms, blockers, or shame. Swift + SwiftUI, menu-bar app, local-first data, bring-your-own-AI.

- Peripheral Chromatic Time Ticker — feel time pass without a countdown (time blindness)
- Holographic Goal Sticky — the goal never leaves your screen (object permanence)
- Forgiveness Protocol — verb-first nudges, zero shame markers (RSD-safe)
- Focus Buddy — an AI agent that proposes, and never acts without your approval
